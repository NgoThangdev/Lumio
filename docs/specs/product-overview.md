# Lumio — Product Overview

## Problem

Managing household essentials often creates unnecessary mental overhead. People have to remember what is available at home, what is running low, what may expire soon, and what should be purchased next. If tracking requires too much manual effort, the system quickly becomes outdated and stops being useful.

Lumio aims to reduce that burden by helping the household remember, organize, and surface what matters at the right time. It should warn users when important items are running low or nearing expiry and automatically suggest what should be added to the next shopping list.

## Target users

Lumio is designed for households of two or more people who share groceries, food, personal-care products, cleaning supplies, and other recurring household essentials.

It is especially useful in situations where the household makes larger shopping trips only around twice per month or lives relatively far from major shopping locations, making forgotten or unexpectedly depleted items more inconvenient. These are common use cases for Lumio, not requirements for using the product.

## Core loop

**Stock → Use → Predict → Alert → Plan shopping → Buy → Restock → Stock**

The loop should require as little user effort as possible while keeping the household inventory accurate enough to support useful decisions.

## Product principles

Lumio should prioritize reducing user effort over achieving perfect inventory precision.

The product should be quick, forgiving, predictable, explainable, and easy to maintain. Routine actions should require minimal input, known information should not be requested again, and the interface should progressively reveal complexity only when needed.

The application should surface what requires attention rather than overwhelm users with data. It should use sensible defaults and automation wherever possible while keeping important decisions understandable and under the user's control.

Above all, Lumio should feel like a lightweight household assistant, not warehouse or ERP software.
